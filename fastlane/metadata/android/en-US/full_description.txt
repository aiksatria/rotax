ROTAX is a native, non-root Android utility designed to give users system-wide control over display orientation (Portrait, Landscape, Auto) using official Android public system settings APIs.

Features:
- Pure Non-Root System Rotation Override.
- Floating Overlay Controller widget over other apps.
- Quick Settings Tile integration.
- Persistent Notification Control Shade.
- Safe Restore (Compare-Before-Restore) to prevent settings conflicts.
- 100% Offline, Zero Ads, Zero Trackers (No INTERNET permission requested).
- Built with Jetpack Compose Material 3 & Kotlin Coroutines.
